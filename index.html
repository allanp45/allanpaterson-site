About

Built in the field, not in a classroom.

For more than 40 years I've worked in B2B sales across New Zealand and Australia: territory management, key accounts and business development. For 13 of those years I ran my own businesses, with all the highs, setbacks and lessons that come with it.

I've learned that what holds most people back after 40 isn't ability. It's clarity, a plan, and someone in their corner who has actually been there.

Today I coach ambitious professionals and business owners over 40, and I build my own ventures in health and recruitment. I share what works, and what doesn't, as I go.
